# Contributing

Corrections and carefully sourced improvements are welcome. This repository is a public-source research resource, not a live threat feed.

## What to include

- Identify the file, section, and specific statement you are addressing.
- Link to the original publication or advisory, and include its publication date. Prefer MITRE ATT&CK, government advisories, and primary vendor research.
- Separate directly reported facts from assessment, and preserve uncertainty where sources disagree.
- For indicators, include provenance and observation dates. Do not present stale indicators as current detections.
- Keep changes educational and defensive. Do not submit exploit payloads, malware, stolen data, or instructions for compromising systems.

## Open an issue or pull request

Use GitHub Issues for suggested corrections, missing citations, and questions about the report. Use a pull request for a focused change and explain its evidence in the description. Please avoid duplicating private, sensitive, or non-public incident details.

## Rights

Submitting a contribution does not change the rights status of the report text, extracted figures, or quoted third-party sources. Do not add material unless you have the right to share it, and do not assume that repository content is available under an open license.
